ABOUT KANAVAGAM

A Home Filled with Love, Care & Hope

Kanavagam Children's Home is a safe and loving home for orphaned and destitute girls. We believe that every child deserves a family, quality education, emotional support, and the opportunity to dream without fear. Our mission is to provide a nurturing environment where every child grows with confidence, dignity, and hope for a brighter future.
