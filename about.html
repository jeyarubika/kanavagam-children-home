OUR MISSION

What We Believe

❤️ Love & Care

Every child deserves unconditional love, emotional support, and a safe family environment where they feel valued and protected.

🎓 Quality Education

We provide access to quality education, helping every child develop knowledge, confidence, and the skills needed for a successful future.

🌱 Empowerment

We encourage every child to dream big, become independent, and grow into compassionate and responsible members of society.

Who We Are

Kanavagam Children's Home is a safe and loving home for orphaned and destitute girls. Founded by Balaji Prakash Rao and Kavitha, Kanavagam provides every child with care, education, healthcare, emotional support, and the opportunity to build a bright future in a family environment.

Our Vision

We dream of a world where every child grows with love, dignity, education, and equal opportunities to achieve their dreams.
